How Road to 180 works

This app rewards showing up, not perfection. No streak resets. No red failure screens. No earning food with exercise.

Apple Watch steps and exercise minutes are context. Your personal trend matters more than an arbitrary daily step number.

Starting targets

1,600–1,700 calories90–110 g proteinWater throughout the day

These are guideposts, not pass/fail numbers. We adjust based on your trend, hunger, energy and how you feel.

Budget-friendly rhythm

Breakfast: eggs most days; bagel days can stay.

Lunch/dinner: chicken pasta stretched with vegetables, eggs, tuna, potatoes, beans or leftovers.

Snacks: boiled eggs, carrots, yogurt, or 1–2 small Kodiak protein balls.

Puffiness notes

Use the 0–3 score and notes to look for patterns with sodium-heavy meals, sleep, stress and activity.

This tracker can't diagnose swelling. Seek medical advice for new or worsening generalized swelling, rapid weight gain over a few days, shortness of breath, reduced urination, or significant leg swelling. Lip, tongue or throat swelling or trouble breathing is an emergency.
