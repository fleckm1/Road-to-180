Puffiness experiment

Track puffiness 0–3 and note especially salty/packaged meals. Don't use this tracker to diagnose swelling.

Seek medical care promptly for new/worsening generalized swelling, rapid weight gain over a few days, shortness of breath, reduced urination, or significant leg swelling. Lip/tongue/throat swelling or trouble breathing is an emergency.
